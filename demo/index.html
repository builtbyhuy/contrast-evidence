What this check can tell you

A closer look.
A careful conclusion.

One background color doesn’t describe a photograph. This playground checks every background pixel in the rectangle around the preview text and marks the lowest contrast.

Below the target means “needs review.” The weakest pixel may fall between letters. Inspect the background directly behind the letters before deciding whether the text fails.

Above the target means “quick check clear.” All sampled pixels meet the selected text threshold in this controlled preview. It does not certify an entire page or cover every accessibility requirement.
